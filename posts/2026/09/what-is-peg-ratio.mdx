## What Is PEG Ratio?

The PEG Ratio, or Price/Earnings-to-Growth Ratio, compares a company's P/E ratio with an earnings-growth rate.

It attempts to add a growth perspective to the traditional P/E ratio.

For the basics of valuation, see [What Is P/E Ratio? A Beginner's Guide](https://disciplinetrading.com/blog/what-is-pe-ratio).

## PEG Ratio Formula

A simplified formula is:

**PEG Ratio = P/E Ratio ÷ Earnings Growth Rate**

Suppose a company has a P/E Ratio of 30 and expected earnings growth of 20%.

**PEG Ratio = 30 ÷ 20 = 1.5**

Different data providers can use different growth estimates and methodologies, so investors should check the definition before comparing PEG values.

## Why Do Investors Use PEG Ratio?

P/E tells investors how much the market is paying relative to earnings.

Two companies can have the same P/E while having very different growth expectations.

PEG attempts to bring those two ideas together.

A P/E of 30 may look expensive for a slow-growing company but could be interpreted differently for a business expected to grow earnings substantially.

## Is a PEG Below 1 Always Cheap?

No.

A commonly cited rule of thumb is that a PEG below 1 may indicate valuation is low relative to the growth assumption.

But this should never be treated as an automatic buy signal.

Growth estimates can be wrong, earnings growth may not be sustainable, and the quality of that growth matters.

## PEG Ratio vs P/E Ratio

**P/E:** How much is the market paying for earnings?

**PEG:** How does that valuation compare with an earnings-growth assumption?

PEG builds on P/E rather than replacing it.

## PEG and Growth Quality

Not all earnings growth is equally valuable.

Investors should ask:

- Is revenue growing?
- Are profit margins stable?
- Is cash flow supporting earnings?
- Is growth coming from the core business?
- Is debt increasing rapidly?
- Can the company maintain the growth rate?

For cash-flow analysis, see [What Is Free Cash Flow? A Beginner's Guide](https://disciplinetrading.com/blog/what-is-free-cash-flow).

## A Simple Comparison

**Company A**
- P/E: 25
- Growth estimate: 10%
- PEG: 2.5

**Company B**
- P/E: 30
- Growth estimate: 25%
- PEG: 1.2

Company B has the higher P/E, but its PEG is lower because its assumed earnings growth is higher.

That does not automatically make Company B the better investment.

## Limitations of PEG Ratio

PEG has several limitations:

- Growth estimates can be inaccurate.
- Different analysts can produce different growth forecasts.
- One-time earnings changes can distort growth.
- Mature businesses may not suit growth-based comparisons.
- Cyclical companies can produce misleading growth rates.
- PEG does not directly measure balance-sheet risk.

## PEG and ROE

Investors can combine growth valuation with profitability measures such as [What Is ROE? A Beginner's Guide](https://disciplinetrading.com/blog/what-is-roe).

A company showing high expected growth but weak returns on capital deserves closer investigation.

## How Investors Can Use PEG

A disciplined approach is:

1. Understand the business model.
2. Review historical earnings.
3. Examine the P/E ratio.
4. Understand the growth estimate.
5. Calculate or verify PEG.
6. Compare with similar companies.
7. Examine margins, ROE, debt and cash flow.
8. Test whether the growth assumption appears realistic.

## Final Thoughts

PEG Ratio can help investors connect valuation with earnings growth.

Its main strength is that it adds context to P/E. Its main weakness is that the result depends heavily on the quality of the growth estimate.

Use PEG as one part of a broader valuation framework rather than as a standalone signal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does PEG stand for?

PEG stands for Price/Earnings-to-Growth Ratio.

### Is PEG below 1 always good?

No. A low PEG can result from an overly optimistic growth assumption.

### Is PEG better than P/E?

Not necessarily. They provide different information and can be used together.

### Can PEG be used for every company?

It is generally more useful when meaningful earnings-growth estimates are available.
