## Open Interest (OI) Analysis

Total Call OI stands at 994.04 lakh contracts against Total Put OI of 784.67 lakh contracts, with Call OI running well ahead of Put OI for this expiry. ## Change in OI Analysis

Looking at fresh Open Interest additions for the day rather than the cumulative total gives a read on where positioning is actively building right now.

Fresh Call and Put writing are both heaviest right around current spot, in the 22,600–22,700 band, with roughly matched intensity on both sides there. On the Call side, a second strong build-up appears at 23,000, extending the resistance case further out. Put-side additions taper off quickly beyond the near-spot zone.

This suggests active two-way positioning right at spot this week, while Call writers are additionally reinforcing the more distant 23,000 resistance with fresh conviction that Put writers aren't matching on the downside.

## Put-Call Ratio (PCR) Analysis

The current PCR for this expiry is 0.80, meaning Call OI outweighs Put OI by a moderate margin.

A PCR in this range generally reflects a meaningful, though not extreme, tilt toward Call writing over Put writing. Since option writers typically sell options they expect to expire worthless, a PCR of 0.80 is conventionally read as a mildly bearish-to-neutral signal, with Call sellers positioning for NIFTY to stay capped below their strikes.

That said, PCR is one data point among several, and a single week's reading should be considered alongside price action and the broader OI structure rather than in isolation.
