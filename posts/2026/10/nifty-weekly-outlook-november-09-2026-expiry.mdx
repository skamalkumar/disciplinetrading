## Open Interest (OI) Analysis

Total Call OI stands at 873.87 lakh contracts against Total Put OI of 996.04 lakh contracts, with Put OI moving ahead of Call OI for this expiry after last week's Call-heavy skew. The OI distribution across strikes shows clear concentration zones on both sides:

## Change in OI Analysis

Looking at fresh Open Interest additions for the day rather than the cumulative total gives a read on where positioning is actively building right now.

Fresh Put writing is heaviest at the 23,600 strike, with further large additions at 23,500 and 23,700, reinforcing the 23,500–23,700 band as the key support area for the week. Fresh Call writing is concentrated further out, with the largest additions at 24,200 and 24,300, followed by 23,600 and 24,000, while some Call unwinding is visible at a few lower strikes around 23,300.

This lines up with the cumulative OI picture: writers are building a floor in the 23,500–23,700 band, while Call writers are pushing their fresh positions higher, to the 24,200–24,300 area.

## Put-Call Ratio (PCR) Analysis

The current PCR for this expiry is 1.13, meaning Put OI modestly outweighs Call OI.

A PCR just above 1 generally reflects a slight tilt toward Put writing over Call writing, without being as skewed as a reading well above 1.4. Since option writers typically sell options they expect to expire worthless, a PCR in this range is conventionally read as a mildly bullish-to-neutral signal, with Put sellers showing a modest lean toward NIFTY holding above their strikes. This is a swing back above 1 from the Call-heavy 0.88 reading on the previous NIFTY expiry.

That said, PCR is one data point among several, and a single week's reading should be considered alongside price action and the broader OI structure rather than in isolation.
