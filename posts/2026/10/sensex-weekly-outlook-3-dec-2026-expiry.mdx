## Open Interest (OI) Analysis

Open interest shows how many contracts remain open at each strike. Reading the [option chain](https://www.disciplinetrading.com/blog/what-is-option-chain) by strike shows where option writers have concentrated their positions, and those strikes often act as reference zones.

On the put side, the largest OI sits at the 84,000 strike at roughly 3.8 L. A smaller put cluster is visible at 83,500 (about 1.05 L), and puts at 84,300 and 84,500 stand at roughly 1.0 L and 1.65 L. The 84,000 strike is the major support zone for this expiry, with 83,500 as the deeper cushion.

On the call side, the heaviest OI is at 84,000 (about 2.75 L) and 84,500 (about 2.25 L), with another notable cluster at 85,000 (about 1.55 L) and a smaller one at 84,300 (about 1.25 L). The 84,500 to 85,000 region is the main resistance area, and 85,000 is the strike that stands out on the upside.

Spot at 84,328.59 sits between the 84,000 put base and the 84,500 call build, so the index is trading inside a clearly defined OI range.

## Change in OI Analysis

Change in OI shows where fresh positions were added or unwound. It tells you which levels the market is building around and which it is leaving.

The largest fresh addition is on the put side at 84,000 (about 2.55 L), which strengthens that strike as the key support base. Put additions are also visible at 84,500 (about 1.45 L), 84,300 (about 0.95 L) and 84,100 (about 0.5 L).

On the call side, the biggest addition is at 84,500 (about 1.8 L), followed by 84,300 (about 1.2 L), 84,000 (about 1.0 L), and 85,000 (about 0.65 L). Fresh call writing at 84,500 and 85,000 reinforces the overhead resistance region. Small call unwinding shows at 83,500 and 83,800, which are well below the current level and carry little weight.

Fresh positions are building on both sides around 84,000 to 84,500, which shows the market is defining a range rather than leaning one way.

## Put-Call Ratio (PCR) Analysis

PCR is Total Put OI divided by Total Call OI. At 1.08, Put OI is slightly higher than Call OI. A reading just above 1 shows put writing at least matching call writing, which generally points to a balanced to mildly supportive positioning backdrop. It does not predict direction on its own.

PCR also changes as expiry nears, because OI is unwound and [time decay in options](https://www.disciplinetrading.com/blog/what-is-time-decay-in-options) erodes premiums. It is best read together with the strike-level OI picture above rather than as a standalone number.
