## Open Interest (OI) Analysis

Total Call OI stands at 830.55 lakh contracts against Total Put OI of 939.18 lakh contracts, with Put OI running modestly ahead of Call OI for this expiry. If you're not familiar with how Open Interest is built up and interpreted, [What Is Open Interest? The OI distribution across strikes shows a distinct pattern this week:

- The 25,000 strike, just below current spot, carries the heaviest Put OI on the chain and a near-equal Call OI build-up, making it the key pivot zone for the week.
- The heaviest Call OI on the entire chain sits at the 25,500 strike, well above spot, marking the primary resistance zone. Secondary Call concentrations appear at 25,400 and 25,200.
- Below the pivot, Put OI is spread across several secondary clusters at 24,800, 24,900 and 24,600, forming the broader support zone.

## Change in OI Analysis

Looking at fresh Open Interest additions for the day rather than the cumulative total gives a read on where positioning is actively building right now.

Fresh writing is heaviest at the 25,000 strike on both sides, with Put additions clearly ahead of Call additions, reinforcing it as the key zone to watch this week. Further fresh Put additions appear at 24,900, 24,800 and 24,600, while the next large Call addition is at 25,500, with smaller additions at 24,900 and 25,200.

This lines up with the cumulative OI picture: option writers are converging on 25,000 as the pivotal level for the week, with Put-side conviction building ahead of Call-side conviction around it, and Call writers separately reinforcing the 25,500 ceiling.

## Put-Call Ratio (PCR) Analysis

The current PCR for this expiry is 1.13, meaning Put OI modestly outweighs Call OI.

A PCR just above 1 generally reflects a slight tilt toward Put writing over Call writing, without being as skewed as a reading well above 1.4. Since option writers typically sell options they expect to expire worthless, a PCR in this range is conventionally read as a mildly bullish-to-neutral signal, with Put sellers showing a modest lean toward NIFTY holding above their strikes. The reading is unchanged from the previous NIFTY expiry.

That said, PCR is one data point among several, and a single week's reading should be considered alongside price action and the broader OI structure rather than in isolation.
