## Open Interest (OI) Analysis

Open interest shows how many contracts remain open at each strike. Reading the [option chain](https://www.disciplinetrading.com/blog/what-is-option-chain) by strike shows where option writers have concentrated their positions, and those strikes often act as reference zones.

On the call side, the heaviest OI sits at the 25,500 strike at roughly 45 L, and another large cluster is at 25,800 at roughly 45 L. Strikes at 25,400, 25,600 and 25,700 each carry about 28 L to 30 L. Heavy call writing above the current level makes the 25,500 to 25,800 region the main resistance area for this expiry.

On the put side, the largest OI is at the 25,000 strike at roughly 40 L. Further support is visible at 25,200 (about 21 L) and 25,300 (about 19 L). The 25,000 strike is the major support zone, with 25,200 and 25,300 as nearer cushions beneath the current level.

Spot at 25,349.65 sits between the 25,300 put base and the 25,400 to 25,500 call wall, so the index is trading inside a clearly defined OI range.

## Change in OI Analysis

Change in OI shows where fresh positions were added or unwound. It tells you which levels the market is building around and which it is leaving.

Fresh call additions are strongest at 25,500 (about 27 L) and 25,800 (about 26 L). Additions of about 20 L to 21 L at 25,600 and 25,700 follow, with a smaller build of around 12 L at 25,400. Call writers are adding mainly above the current level, which reinforces the 25,500 to 25,800 resistance region.

On the put side, the largest fresh addition is at 25,000 (about 14.5 L), with smaller additions at 25,400 and 25,500 (about 10 L to 12 L). The 25,000 call strike shows some unwinding, which fits with fresh put writing at the same strike. The market is adding to the 25,000 support base, while the build at 25,400 and 25,500 shows put writers also operating closer to spot.

## Put-Call Ratio (PCR) Analysis

PCR is Total Put OI divided by Total Call OI. At 0.69, Put OI is lower than Call OI. A reading below 1 means call writing outweighs put writing, which usually signals caution and resistance overhead. It does not predict direction on its own.

PCR also changes as expiry nears, because OI is unwound and [time decay in options](https://www.disciplinetrading.com/blog/what-is-time-decay-in-options) erodes premiums. It is best read together with the strike-level OI picture above rather than as a standalone number.
