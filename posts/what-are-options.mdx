## What Are Options?

Options are financial contracts that give the buyer the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell an underlying asset at a predetermined price before a specific date.

In India, the most popular options are on Nifty 50 and BankNifty indices.

## Call Options

A Call Option gives you the right to buy the underlying asset at the strike price. You buy a Call when you expect the market to go UP.

## Put Options

A Put Option gives you the right to sell the underlying asset at the strike price. You buy a Put when you expect the market to go DOWN.
